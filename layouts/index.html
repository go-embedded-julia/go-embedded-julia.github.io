Julia, embedded in Go.
An interpreter that runs Julia's own Base.

go-embedded-julia is in development: milestone M0 (the oracle harness) and milestone M1 (the parser). It will be a pure-Go, embeddable Julia interpreter: JuliaSyntax and JuliaLowering ported to Go, and an interpreter over the lowered IR that runs Julia's Base unchanged, for the julia cells of go-notebook and its WebAssembly playground. Nothing is released.

An interpreter, and honest about it.

It keeps Julia's semantics, not its native speed. An interpreter cannot match Julia's JIT on loops, so heavy computation goes through the Go libraries (go-ndarray, go-bignum, go-fft). The comparisons that can hold are start-up and first result, and the kernels routed to those libraries. The plan lists every interpreter speed statement as unverified: nothing has been run yet.
